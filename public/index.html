Model notes

A simplified 3.0-second earthquake shakes the ground and starts the tower moving. After that, the foundation stops and the graph shows free motion. Sway means roof displacement relative to the foundation, x - y.

T = 2*pi*sqrt(L/g) and L = g*(T/2*pi)^2

Two hydraulic dampers remove mechanical energy from pendulum motion. The thermal energy readout is cumulative dissipated energy. At zero damping, the hydraulic dampers are removed.

Wall impacts use a simple restitution model: the bob is placed at the wall and its angular velocity reverses with reduced speed.

Failure rule: Easy has no danger-zone collapse. Medium fails at 3.0 s of accumulated danger time. Hard fails at 2.5 s. Danger time starts after the earthquake and builds while roof sway is beyond the tower's game sway limit, shown above. This tolerance varies by tower to keep challenges achievable; it is not a real structural prediction.
